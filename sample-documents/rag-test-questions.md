# Suggested RAG Test Questions

Use these questions after ingesting the sample policy documents:

1. Who approves privileged access requests?
2. Where must production credentials be stored?
3. What reviews are required before a generative AI application goes to production?
4. Why are source passages preserved in RAG responses?
5. What should happen after a significant AI incident?
6. Who validates the requested privilege level?
7. What happens to access that is no longer required?

Also test an unrelated question such as "What is the company's vacation policy?" to verify that the application does not invent unsupported information.
